## Summary by section

### Organisation & mechanism
- Hack2skill runs the hackathon. Registering means you accept the terms.
- The organiser may cancel, postpone or reschedule, and may amend the terms without notice.
  If the event is cancelled, personal expenses aren't refunded.
- Registration goes through the microsite only. The organiser isn't liable for late, lost,
  damaged or incomplete entries, or for technical failures.
- Signing up means agreeing to receive event emails and messages.

### Teams, attendance & presentation
- A team has 2–6 members, and each person can be in only one team. The team name is chosen
  at registration.
- All members must be present online at checkpoints and on the defined communication
  platforms (for example Slack, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp). Once the event starts, leaving
  the platform or dashboard can lead to disqualification.
- Selected teams present for 5 minutes, including a live demo of at least 30 seconds and Q&A.

### Judging
- A panel of judges decides the winners. Their decisions, and the organiser's, are final.
  Entrants release the organiser and sponsors from liability over judging decisions.
- The general criteria are **Impact**, **Creativity**, **Design** (blueprint, crash-proof front
  end, UI/UX) and **Execution** (clear timeline, effective results). They are "not limited
  to" these four. The problem-specific judging focus is listed in
  [problem-statement.md](problem-statement.md#judging-focus).

### Prizes & awards
- Prizes are listed on the hackathon website and may change without notice. They can't be
  transferred.
- Winners are responsible for any taxes. Cash prizes are paid within 60 days, with TDS
  deducted as the rules require.

### Conduct, code of conduct & anti-harassment
- Follow the law. Don't create unlawful or objectionable content.
- The event is inclusive and harassment-free, online and offline. That includes hacks, talks
  and social media. Anyone asked to stop harassing behaviour must stop immediately.
- Don't photograph people who object, or anyone in a private context.
- Report incidents to the organising committee. Violations can lead to warnings or expulsion.

### Intellectual property
- The organiser gets a **6-month right of first refusal** on exclusive licensing or sale of the
  submitted materials (software, source code, documentation, data, concepts). If a third party
  makes an offer, the organiser has 30 days to match it.
- The organiser may publish and disclose submitted ideas in any medium, worldwide, free of
  charge.
- Results must not infringe third-party IP. Participants indemnify the organiser against
  infringement claims.
- By submitting, participants warrant that:
  - they hold the rights to what they submit
  - they aren't publishing falsehoods that harm others
  - the content isn't unlawful or offensive
  - it contains no ads or solicitations
  - it contains no malware
- Participants also grant a perpetual, non-exclusive marketing licence for content derived from
  the entry.
- **Copyright:** the submission must be original, or sufficiently licensed, and malware-free.

### Eligibility & data
- The organiser decides eligibility. It may disqualify entries that violate third-party
  rights or the law, use inappropriate language or images, or are otherwise objectionable.
- Only complete entries that meet the technical requirements are accepted.
- Participants aged 14–17 need a guardian or a signed minor waiver.
- Data security follows the IT Act, as referenced in the terms.
- These terms override any conflicting promotional material.
